Redwoods to Yosemite Loop

One loop out of San Francisco: up the coast to the redwoods, east to Lassen, down to Tahoe, over Tioga Pass into Yosemite, then home. One must-do hike a day, none much over 3,000 ft of climbing.

Route: San Francisco → Marin → Redwood NP → Lassen → Lake Tahoe → Tuolumne / Yosemite → SF
Totals: about 1,440 miles and 35 hours of driving, 70 trail miles, 14,200 ft of climbing. Highest point 10,457 ft (Lassen Peak).

Check the week of the trip: Glacier Point Road is closed because of the Dome Fire near Wawona, with no reopening date, so this plan skips Glacier Point. Tioga Pass (Hwy 120) closes with the first big snow. If it closes before Oct 22, use the fallback plan at the bottom.

When to hit the trailheads

Hike times at your measured pace, from two AllTrails recordings (Slacker Hill after about 10 flat miles, and Filbert Steps): about 2.0 mph uphill on normal trail grades, 1.5 mph on steep stairs, 2.7 mph on flats and descents. Also built in: 5% slower with a loaded pack, 10% slower above 9,000 ft, and about 10 minutes of breaks per hour. Sunrise is around 7:15–7:35 and sunset around 6:10–6:30 all week.

Day by day

Sat 17 · San Francisco

Lands End Trail · 3.4 mi · 541 ft gain

Arrives around 1pm. Lunch in North Beach, then the coastal trail from Sutro Baths past Mile Rock to Eagle's Point. Then drive 10 minutes to the Presidio for Batteries to Bluffs (1.6 mi, 285 ft): a steep stairway down to a wild beach with the Golden Gate Bridge right in front of you. Watch sunset from the bluffs.
Sleep: home

Sun 18 · Marin → north coast

Steep Ravine, Dipsea & Matt Davis Loop · 6.8 mi · 1,699 ft gain

Redwood creek canyon with a wooden ladder, then open ridges above Stinson Beach. It's a Sunday, so get to Pantoll by 8am. On the way, stop at Battery Spencer for the classic close-up of the Golden Gate Bridge (5 min off 101). Lunch in Mill Valley, then drive north on 101.
Drive 1 hr to Pantoll, then 7 hr to Klamath (8 hr total) · Sleep: Klamath

Mon 19 · Redwood NP → Lassen

James Irvine → Fern Canyon → Clintonia → Miner's Ridge · 11.2 mi · 1,581 ft gain

Old-growth redwoods out to Fern Canyon's 50-foot fern walls, then back over the ridge. Trail notes (NPS, Oct 2026): the eastern stairs between the James Irvine Trail and Fern Canyon are closed for construction, so use the western stairs. A small tree is down on James Irvine but passable. Miner's Ridge is blocked past the Clintonia junction, which is why this loop uses Clintonia. Don't follow Miner's Ridge down to the beach. In the afternoon, Hwy 299 east to Redding, then Hwy 44 up to Shingletown.
Drive 30 min to the trailhead, then 4 hr 15 min to Shingletown · Sleep: Shingletown

Tue 20 · Lassen → Tahoe

Lassen Peak (4.9 mi) + Bumpass Hell (3 mi) · 2,426 ft gain

Leave Shingletown at dawn, about 1 hr to the trailhead through the north entrance and summit the volcano early. On a clear day Mt Shasta fills the view about 75 miles north. Then the boardwalks over Bumpass Hell's steaming vents, and south toward Tahoe. Bring microspikes if there's been snow. If the Lassen Peak trail is icy or closed, do Brokeoff Mountain instead (7.3 mi, 2,549 ft, tops out 1,200 ft lower), then Bumpass Hell if there's time.
Drive 1 hr to the trailhead, then 4 hr 40 min to Tahoe · Sleep: South Lake Tahoe

Wed 21 · Lake Tahoe

Emerald Bay to D.L. Bliss via the Rubicon Trail · 10.5 mi · 1,456 ft gain

The shoreline trail along Lake Tahoe's clearest water: down to the Vikingsholm castle on Emerald Bay, past the lighthouse, to the coves at D.L. Bliss and back. Park at the Vikingsholm lot early. Optional add-on: the short climb from Eagle Falls trailhead, across the road, to Eagle Lake (free Desolation Wilderness day permit at the trailhead). Stretch option for a view of the whole lake from above: Mt Tallac (9.9 mi, 3,287 ft).
Drive 25 min each way · Sleep: South Lake Tahoe

Thu 22 · Tahoe → Tuolumne

Cathedral Lakes · 9.6 mi · 1,663 ft gain

Leave by 7am: Hwy 89 through Hope Valley, over Monitor Pass, 395 south, over Tioga Pass (this scenic route is about 21 min longer than the fastest one). At the trailhead by about 10:45, after Tioga Road's construction delays. Sunset at Mono Lake's South Tufa, then 25 min south to June Lake.
Drive 3 hr 20 min to the trailhead, 55 min to Mono Lake, 25 min to June Lake · Sleep: June Lake

Fri 23 · Yosemite high country

Clouds Rest · 12.6 mi · 3,038 ft gain

The best hike of the trip: a granite ridge at 9,926 ft, about 1,000 ft above Half Dome, with all of Yosemite Valley below. Leave June Lake by 6:35 to be on the trail by 7:45. Afterward, drive down into Yosemite Valley and catch sunset at Tunnel View (around 6:15).
Drive 1 hr 10 min to the trailhead, 1 hr 25 min to Tunnel View, then 1 hr 40 min to Mariposa. Take Hwy 140 through El Portal, not Wawona Rd (Hwy 41), which passes the Dome Fire · Sleep: Mariposa

Sat 24 · Yosemite Valley → SFO

Mist Trail and Clark Point loop · 4.7 mi · 1,535 ft gain

Up the Mist Trail's granite staircase beside Vernal Fall, then back down the John Muir Trail past Clark Point, which looks straight at Nevada Fall. It's the Valley classic without the risk of missing the flight the day after Clouds Rest. The flight leaves SFO at 7:11pm, so the day runs backward from that:

  • 6:10am leave Mariposa (1 hr 50 min to Happy Isles trailhead)
  • 8:00am start hiking (about 2 hr 55 min at your pace)
  • ~11:00am off the trail; walk to Lower Yosemite Fall (1.2 mi, flat) and stop at El Capitan Meadow
  • 12:15pm leave the Valley, no later.
  • 5:40pm at SFO (4 hr 40 min drive plus about 45 min for traffic and food)

Feeling strong? The full Vernal and Nevada Falls loop (6.8 mi, 2,070 ft, about 4h15) only fits if you leave Mariposa at 5:40, start by 7:30, and reach the top of Vernal Fall by 8:45.

Before you go

  • If Tioga Pass closes: stay a third night in Tahoe, enter Yosemite from the west on Hwy 120, and spend both Yosemite days in the Valley: the Mist Trail plus Four Mile Trail to Union Point (6.8 mi, 2,385 ft). Stay in Yosemite West or Mariposa both nights.
  • Altitude: you sleep at 6,300 ft or lower until June Lake (7,600 ft) on night six, by which point you'll have adjusted. Day trips to 10,000 ft are fine. Turn around if a headache keeps getting worse or you get nausea.
  • Snow: the top of Lassen Peak and Clouds Rest can be icy by late October. Microspikes are about $70 at REI.
  • Yosemite Plan B (smoke in the high country or tired legs): decide around Oct 20 from the daily trip-watch email. Swap Friday to the full Vernal and Nevada Falls loop in the Valley (its upper section is open Fri–Sun), then make Saturday easy: Mirror Lake (5.1 mi, 341 ft) and Lower Yosemite Fall. This was AllTrails Trip Assistant's own plan.
  • Smoke and permits: check fire.airnow.gov the night before each Yosemite hike. The Dome Fire is about 10 miles south of the Valley, and smoke settles there in the mornings. If the reading is red, skip Clouds Rest. No Yosemite entry reservation in 2026. The only permit is the free Desolation Wilderness day permit for Eagle Lake, filled out at the trailhead.
